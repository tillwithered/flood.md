# Reply in the right channel

Use one MessageComposer with a clearly named channel control. Public replies and internal notes have separate drafts. Key each draft by both record and channel. Switching record, opening a menu or applying a macro must preserve the other draft and its attachments.

Carry the destination channel through the submit request. Have the host validate permissions and accept the exact submitted draft. Clear the accepted draft only after success. If the user edited it during the request, keep the newer content.

Preview only the fields the macro would change. Show the proposed reply and attachments below the field changes. If applying the macro would overwrite unsent content, show the draft that is being replaced and make the destination explicit. Applying the preview populates the selected draft. Sending is the separate action on the composer.

Test switching channels with two different drafts, removing one attachment, rejecting a macro, applying it to the intended draft and retrying a failed send after changing channels.
